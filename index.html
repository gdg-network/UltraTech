Customers can provide their own SD card, USB drive, or SSD. Compatible storage options may be available for an additional cost.

How long does service take?

Most setups take between 24-48 hours depending on the device and the amount of customization requested.

Will my data be safe?

Customers should back up important data before service. We take care to preserve existing files whenever possible.

Are there any discounts?

Discounts may be available in person, including multi-device orders and referral rewards. Ask about current offers when requesting a service.

Do you provide game files?

Yes. We don't recommend it but it is a option. You need a extra 5 bucks for a site with any game, not just games for your console.

Can you upgrade my Steam Deck?

Yes, supported upgrades include storage upgrades, software setup, and optimization assistance.
